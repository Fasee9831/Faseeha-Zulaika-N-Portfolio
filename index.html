Problem Statement

Traditional sentiment analysis tools are limited to single short sentences. Organizations, researchers, and product teams struggle to process hundreds of customer reviews, bulk feedback spreadsheets (Excel, CSV), and multi-page reports (PDF, Word) in one place with actionable business guidance.

Core Solution

Engineered ZendiMind AI, a comprehensive document and text intelligence platform deployed on Vercel. It processes direct text input and batch files up to 32MB, extracting text columns from spreadsheets and documents, evaluating polarity, subjectivity, and emotion drivers, and generating instant recommendations and exportable reports.

Key Features & Innovations

Real-time Sentiment & Polarity Engine

Instant classification into Positive, Neutral, and Negative categories with fine-grained confidence scoring, polarity index, and subjectivity metrics.

Batch Document & Spreadsheet Intelligence

Supports uploading Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, PDF, Word (.docx), and Text files. Features automatic column detection and batch review parsing for thousands of records.

Multi-Class Emotion & Driver Detection

Detects emotional drivers including Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, and Neutral tone to understand customer intent and satisfaction nuance.

Actionable Business Recommendations

Delivers automated, context-aware improvement recommendations based on detected negative patterns to assist product teams in boosting customer retention.

Interactive Cinema Robot Preloader

Custom glassmorphic 3D robot preloader with audio soundscape toggle, particle celebration canvas, and instant light/dark theme switching.

KPI Visualizations & Exportable Reports

Visual distribution charts, interactive review inspection tables, and 1-click export of analyzed datasets directly to CSV.
